# Angles

An angle is the reason the reader should care, said from their side. A
format is how it is shown. A hook is the first line. The campaign tests
angles first, because a wrong angle in a good format still fails, and a
right angle survives a plain format. `references/strategy.md` has the
taxonomy, the matrix, the starter set, the cadence and the refresh
triggers; read it once, then work from the brief.

## The work

1. **Read** `campaigns/<slug>/brief.md`, `brand/voice.md`, `public/proof.md`,
   `public/services.md`, and any `campaigns/*/results.md` (what already
   worked or failed for this business beats any taxonomy).
2. **Claims first.** Write `campaigns/<slug>/claims.md`: every fact the
   campaign might state, numbered, each with its source note and a date.
   Numbers, lead times, materials, prices, quotes, awards, years in
   business. What has no source is a question for the owner, asked once.
   Nothing enters a creative that is not on this list.
3. **Five angles**, one line each, from the five families (outcome,
   problem, social proof, mechanism, comparison), each written as the
   customer would say it, each pointing at the claim it rests on:

   ```
   1. outcome      "A kitchen fitted in three days, by the people who built it."   claims #2, #4
   2. problem      "Six weeks of 'someone will be in touch'."                       claims #4
   3. social proof "The Clifton one: 'they measured twice and it shows.'"          claims #6
   4. mechanism    "Measured, drawn, built and fitted by the same two people."     claims #1, #2
   5. comparison   "Showroom kitchen or workshop kitchen."                          claims #1, #3
   ```

4. **Pick three** for the first batch, with the awareness level in mind
   (cold readers need the problem or the comparison before the mechanism;
   warm readers take proof and the offer). Say why the other two wait.
5. **The 3×3.** For each chosen angle, the format that shows it best and
   two alternatives, from `static-ad/references/formats.md` (or the post
   formats in `social-post/references/`), so the first batch is nine
   distinct concepts, not one concept in nine colours. Write it as a
   table in `angles.md`: angle, format, hook line, template, claim
   numbers.
6. **Show it** to the owner in one message and ask which three to make
   first (or make the three you recommend and say so).

## Rules

- Distinct concepts beat variants: Meta's delivery since Andromeda treats
  a colour change as the same ad. Nine rows in the table should be nine
  different ideas.
- One claim per angle is enough. An angle that needs four claims is a
  page, not an ad.
- Hooks are written later, per creative, in the platform's voice; the
  `hook line` column here is the idea, not the final words.
- Iterate one variable at a time when results come in: the hook first,
  then the format, then the angle (`results` skill).
