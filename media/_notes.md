# What the media shows, and what may be used

One line per file or per folder, written from the owner's answers. A file
with no line here is not used in a creative.

| File | What it shows | People in it | May be used | Asked on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (example) photos/kitchen-clifton-01.jpg | The finished Clifton kitchen, oak and brass | none | yes | to fill |

Rules the AI follows:

- A recognisable person needs the owner's word that the picture may be
  used in advertising, and the customer's if it is their home or their
  face.
- A customer's property is theirs; ask before it appears in an ad.
- A photograph of someone else's work is never used.
- A stock photo the owner downloaded is not the business's own material;
  keep it out of `media/` or mark it plainly here.
