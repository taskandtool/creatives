# Brief: to fill

The brief is written with the owner in one conversation (the `brief`
skill), from the notes first and the owner's answers second, and it is
agreed before the first creative is made. It lives at
`campaigns/<slug>/brief.md`; `campaigns/CURRENT` names the campaign in
hand. Every field above is a fact or a decision; an adjective is neither.
