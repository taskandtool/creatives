# A phone snapshot

The picture that does not look like an ad: a customer's or the owner's
phone photo, one caption, nothing designed. The owner's real snapshots
first; a generated one is illustrative and the record's notes say so.

## Composition for this format
- Held at arm's length, a little tilted, slightly too close.
- The {subject} in {setting} as it really is: a bit of mess, the edge of
  something, real clutter, no styling.
- Phone light: {light} when it is daylight; a flash with a hard shadow
  when it is not. Slight grain, slight overexposure, no grade.
- Copy-space: none inside the picture; the caption chip sits over it.

## Prompt template
A phone photo of {subject} in {setting}, taken at arm's length, a little
tilted and a little too close, the surroundings as they really are with
a bit of mess at the edges. {light}, or a hard flash shadow. {materials}
where they happen to be. Slight grain, no colour grade, nothing styled.
{people}. {never}. {anchor}

## Hooks that fit this picture
One sentence a person would type under their own photo; a moment, a
day count, a question.

## Pitfalls
A "phone photo" that is lit like a studio; a caption that sells; passing
a generated picture off as a customer's.
