# A quiet backdrop

For the formats that render entirely from the template (a note, a
checklist, a listicle, a quote) when the owner wants a scene behind the
card rather than the plain ground. Most of the time the plain ground is
better; ask before generating one.

## Composition for this format
- {setting} out of focus, in shadow or in even light, with nothing
  legible in it.
- No subject; the card is the subject.
- Copy-space: the whole frame is the copy-space; the template puts a full
  scrim over it.

## Prompt template
{setting} out of focus and in even shadow, no single object in focus,
nothing legible, the whole frame quiet. {light}, dimmed. {materials} as
soft shapes only. The scene is empty of people. {never}. {anchor}

## Pitfalls
A busy backdrop that fights the card; a backdrop with text in it; using
this when the plain ground would do.
