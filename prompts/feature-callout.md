# Feature callout

The product or the work, clean, with room around the parts the arrows
will point at. The template draws the arrows and labels; the picture
leaves them space.

## Composition for this format
- The product or the finished {subject} straight on or three-quarter,
  filling two thirds of the frame, on a plain ground.
- The parts to be called out are visible and unobstructed; nothing
  overlaps them.
- Copy-space: the margins on all four sides.

## Prompt template
The {subject} (from the reference image when there is one, kept exactly
as it is) three-quarter on, filling two thirds of the frame on a plain
ground, every part visible and nothing overlapping it. {light}, soft
shadows one way. {materials}. Wide empty margins on all four sides.
{people}. {never}. {anchor}

## Hooks that fit this picture
The non-obvious benefit of one part, in plain words; a material named.

## Pitfalls
Arrows or labels painted by the model; more than three parts called
out; a feature without a benefit in the copy.
