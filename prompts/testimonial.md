# Testimonial

The words are the reviewer's; the picture, when there is one, is the
thing the review is about, never the reviewer. The `quote` template needs
no picture; use this guide only when the quote sits over a scene.

## Composition for this format
- The subject is what the review praises, at rest, after the job: the
  {subject} in the customer's {setting}, tidy, used.
- Camera from where the customer stands every day.
- Copy-space: the lower half is plain (a wall, a floor, a worktop).
- A review card mimic (`screenshot` template) never has a picture behind
  it.

## Prompt template
The {subject} the review is about, in the customer's {setting}, at rest,
after the work, from where the customer stands every day. {light}.
{materials}. The lower half of the frame is a plain surface with nothing
on it. {people}. {never}. {anchor}

## Hooks that fit this picture
The most specific sentence in the review, quoted as written; the
reviewer's first name and area with permission.

## Pitfalls
A portrait standing in for the reviewer; stars painted into the picture;
a review edited to read better.
