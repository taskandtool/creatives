# An illustration

Only when the brand's Imagery block says the brand is drawn, or the idea
cannot be photographed. The drawing carries the idea; the template
carries the words, never speech bubbles the model draws.

## Composition for this format
- One scene, one idea, flat colour and clean line in the style the brand
  names; the brand's colour on one element.
- Room for the words: one plain area of the drawing left empty, named.
- No lettering, no bubbles, no signs with words in them.

## Prompt template
A {style} illustration of {subject} in {setting}, one scene and one idea,
flat colour with clean line, the brand's colour on one element, a plain
empty area for the words. {people}. No lettering or speech bubbles.
{never}. {anchor}

The {style} slot is the drawing style the brand's Imagery block names
(pass --style when it is not there); without one, this format is
off-brand and a photograph is used instead.

## Pitfalls
A cartoon style the brand does not own; cuteness; text drawn by the
model; a drawing standing in for a product that should be photographed.
