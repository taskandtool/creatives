# The reader's environment

One place the reader recognises as theirs, at the moment the problem is
felt, empty of people or with people seen from behind or by their hands.
Behind a big number, a problem statement, a "who it's for", a listicle,
an advertorial.

## Composition for this format
- The subject is {audience_place}: the place where the reader is when the
  need for {offer} shows up.
- The moment is specific: the time of day, the state of the place, the
  one object that tells the story.
- Camera from the doorway or from the reader's own chair.
- Copy-space: one large plain area named in the prompt (a wall, a sky, a
  floor, a dark half of the room) for the template's words.

## Prompt template
{audience_place} at the moment the need for {offer} shows up, one object
telling the story, the rest of the room as it would really be. Camera
from the doorway at eye level. {light}. {materials} where they belong.
One large plain area (a wall, a floor, a sky) with nothing on it for the
words. {people}. {never}. {anchor}

## Hooks that fit this picture
The number from the ledger; the problem in the reader's words; "[job
title or situation], this is for you" without any personal attribute
Meta forbids.

## Pitfalls
A "stressed person" pose; a person facing the camera; a place that could
be anywhere.
