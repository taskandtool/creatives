# Visual identity

The brand's own colours, type, and logo, stated precisely enough to set the
site's theme from (`styles/theme.css`). Colours as 6-digit hex; fonts by
family name. When a Company Brain writes this file, every value is read from
the real site or the owner's material and cited.

## Colours

| Role | Hex | Where it is used today |
|---|---|---|
| Primary (the one action colour) | to fill | to fill |
| Dark | to fill | to fill |
| Light | to fill | to fill |
| Neutral (secondary text) | to fill | to fill |
| Others | to fill | to fill |

## Type

- **Display (headings):** to fill — family, weights, where it is licensed from (Google Fonts, a file, a system font)
- **Body (reading):** to fill

## Logo

- Files in `logo/` (svg preferred): to fill
- Clear space, minimum size, dark and light versions: to fill

## Imagery

What the business's pictures look like, stated so an image prompt can
reuse it (the creatives' style anchor; `static-ad/references/visual-prompts.md`).

- **What the owner's photography shows:** to fill (the work, the place, the product, the material, hands)
- **Settings:** to fill (the workshop, the van, the customer's home, the counter)
- **Light:** to fill (north window, overcast, one lamp; never studio flash)
- **Materials and objects that carry the brand:** to fill (oak, steel, the blue mug, the red van)
- **People:** to fill (none; hands and backs only; the owner by name with permission)
- **Never shows:** to fill (stock smiles, handshakes, a spotless room, purple)
- **Style anchor:** to fill (one paragraph in the vocabulary above, reused at the end of every image prompt; `scripts/imagegen.py --brand` appends it)
