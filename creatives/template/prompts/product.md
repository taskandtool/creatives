# Product on a ground

The product, real, on the brand's ground or in its own setting. The
owner's product photo is the reference; the model changes only the
setting and the light.

## Composition for this format
- One product, the whole of it in frame, a lot of empty ground around it.
- Camera slightly above, close, so the material and the finish read.
- Ground: the brand's colour as a plain painted ground, or the product's
  own {setting} with everything else cleared.
- Copy-space: the margins; the template places the words around the
  product.

## Prompt template
The product from the reference image, kept exactly as it is: the same
label, shape, logo, colour, material and proportions. Placed on a plain
ground in the brand's colour (or in {setting} with the surfaces cleared),
one product only, a lot of empty ground around it. Camera slightly above
and close. {light}, one soft shadow. {never}. {anchor}

## Hooks that fit this picture
The product's one difference, said as a fact; the material; the price
when the ledger has it.

## Pitfalls
A painted label; a second product the business does not sell; a
reflection that invents a room; a lifestyle scene when a plain ground
was asked for.
